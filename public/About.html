About Weathermap

Weathermap's goal is to provide a quick and easy look at the weather information of anywhere in the world, with just a single search!


Weather information in three simple steps!


All you need to do is:

  • Go to the "WeatherMap page.
  • Enter a city name and click the "Search Cities" button
  • Select the correct city from the dropdown menu.
  • Click the "Search Weather" button.

And instantly get data on the current weather in that city!

What is this project about?

WeatherMap is a web-based app capable of displaying valuable weather information such as the current temperature, humidity, and precipitation for any city of the user's choice. This app fetches real-time weather data from the OpenWeather API using city coordinates from that API's geocoder. The app also features an interactive map that places a marker on a searched city.
